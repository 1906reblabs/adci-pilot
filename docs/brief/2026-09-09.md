## What changed and why

1. **Broadband affordability (INF-02) was withdrawn for all three countries — a blocking finding.** The
   values (1.41 / 1.71 / 2.62) came from a May 2021 article reporting A4AI 2020 data, but had been logged as
   2026 and "re-confirmed" for three weeks. This week's reviewers failed them (ZAF-INF-02-03, NGA-INF-02-03,
   KEN-INF-02-03). Infrastructure now rests on INF-01 alone: ZAF 84.3 → 75.6, NGA 72.2 → 56.1, KEN 61.0 → 35.0.
   That is the whole Nigeria and Kenya decline. The published weeks 08-19 to 09-02 contain the error and
   were **not** edited; revision rows are recommended (see red team).
2. **Chainalysis published its 2026 Global Crypto Adoption Index on 23 September** (new method, 117
   countries). Nigeria is #3 (first in P2P and cross-border); **South Africa is #9**, giving FIN-02 its first
   value (93.1; ZAF-FIN-02-01). South Africa's Finance pillar went 7.3 → 45.4, which is the whole South
   Africa gain. Nigeria FIN-02 moves 96.7 → 98.3 but is not a like-for-like trend (NGA-FIN-02-04). Kenya is
   outside the published top 20, so FIN-02 stays missing.

## Other movements
- **Nigeria digital ID:** NIMC reports 142M enrolled (16 Sept), so GOV-01 rises 59.1 → 64.5 on the same
  220M-population assumption (NGA-GOV-01-04). The assumption matters: 200–250M gives roughly 71%–57%.
- **Nigeria internet:** NCC broadband penetration reached 56.1% in May (53.0% earlier), but it counts
  subscriptions, not people (NGA-INF-01-04).
- **South Africa Smart ID:** 34.7M cards issued and about 700,000 via banks since March, with a green-ID
  cut-off about two years out. It could not be put on the same basis as the scored 63.6%, so GOV-01
  stays at 63.6 with confidence lowered to low; it is probably understated.
- **Kenya Maisha Namba:** a fourth week with no published coverage figure; a June "34M captured" statement is
  the national ID register, not Maisha Namba (rejected).
- **Funding context (not scored):** H1 2026 totals Nigeria $254M ($214M equity, continental #1), Kenya $126M,
  South Africa $83M. ECO-02 stays on FY2025 until a full-year figure exists.

## What needs fixing
- Re-source INF-02 from a current ITU/A4AI country table; consider revisions to published weeks.
- Infrastructure is one indicator per country, and not like-for-like (Nigeria subscriptions vs ZAF/KEN surveys).
- GOV-01 and FIN-01 are each measured three different ways across the three countries.
- Some items were not re-searched live this cycle (ZAF INF-01, ECO-01, Kenya FIN-01 and INF-01 surveys).
- The run is labelled 2026-09-09 but was executed 2026-10-01; no runs for 09-16, 09-23 or 09-30 exist in the knowledge base.
