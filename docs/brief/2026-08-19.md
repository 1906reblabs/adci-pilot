# ADCI Intelligence Brief — Week of 2026-08-19

**This is the pilot's first real weekly run.** Everything published in this
repo before today — the 2026-08-18 snapshot, `observations.json`, the
dashboard's prior numbers — was sample/synthetic placeholder data used to
prove the pipeline out, per the project README. There is no genuine
week-over-week trend yet; that starts now.

## Two findings that should temper how much weight to put on these numbers

1. **Kenya's Finance pillar (98.0) measures mobile-money *registration*,
   not usage.** The Communications Authority of Kenya's own report
   flags this figure (KEN-FIN-01-01) as supply-side, not a survey of
   active transactors — exactly what FIN-01 is defined to capture. Both
   the Data Validator and Source Critic caught this independently this
   week.
2. **Nigeria's Finance pillar (98.7) is a derived, not published,
   number.** It converts Chainalysis's well-corroborated #2-of-151
   global crypto-adoption rank into a 0-100 index via a linear formula
   applied downstream — Chainalysis didn't publish that specific score.

Both are flagged **blocking** in this week's Red Team report
(`red_team.md`), with a specific recommendation to re-run once better
evidence is sourced. Nothing was hand-edited to compensate — per the
project's constitution, only `engine/scoring.py` computes scores, and
correcting these means re-running step 5 with better inputs, not
adjusting the output.

## What's working

- **Infrastructure** is the best-covered pillar across all three
  countries (2 of 2 indicators each), and tells a coherent story: Nigeria
  has the cheapest mobile data of the three (NGA-INF-02-01, 1.71% of
  income), Kenya the most expensive (KEN-INF-02-01, 2.62%, missing the UN
  affordability target), and that ordering roughly tracks each country's
  internet-usage figure.
- **South Africa's digital-ID migration** is a real, concrete GOV↔Finance
  integration point this week: Smart ID issuance now runs substantially
  through retail bank branches (FNB, Capitec, Standard Bank) — see
  `integration_notes.md`.
- **Nigeria's stablecoin/crypto leadership** (global #2 by Chainalysis
  rank) and its **only-country VC decline** (-3% YoY, NGA-ECO-02-01) look
  like two faces of the same underlying naira-volatility story — worth
  watching whether they move together as the currency stabilizes or not.

## What needs fixing next cycle

- **South Africa's Finance pillar is completely empty**, despite a
  mature banking and crypto sector — this looks like a Source Scout
  sampling gap this cycle, not real data unavailability (see Red Team
  Finding 3). Notably, this is the *opposite* of the bias the pilot was
  watching for: South Africa has the strongest statistical base of the
  three countries and the *most* missing data this week.
- **GOV-02, ECO-01, and AI-02 came up empty for all three countries** —
  a 100% miss rate uniform enough to suggest either a Scout coverage gap
  or that these indicators need better-specified public data sources
  (Red Team Finding 4).
