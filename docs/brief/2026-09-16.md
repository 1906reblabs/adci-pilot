## What the checks found

- **Broadband affordability (INF-02) stays withdrawn for all three countries (blocking, unresolved).** The old values were A4AI 2020 data from an article dated 4 May 2021; this run fetched the article and confirmed it. No current Tier-1 country value could be found (ITU's 2025 page gives only global and regional medians; a 2026 Nigeria figure of 0.23% is a vendor calculation on a different basis and was rejected). Infrastructure rests on INF-01 alone. The published weeks 08-19 to 09-02 still carry the error and are unrevised; revision rows are recommended (Red Team Finding 1).
- **South Africa's lead depends on one value.** Its 55.0 includes FIN-02 (rank 9 of 117 on Chainalysis's 2026 index, published 2026-09-23, new method). Without it South Africa would score about 47, below Nigeria. Nigeria's FIN-02 (rank 3 of 117, 98.3) is not a trend either: the edition changed method and country set (ZAF-FIN-02-02, NGA-FIN-02-05; Red Team Finding 2).
- **Nigeria's digital-ID figure is sensitive.** NIMC reports more than 142M enrolled (NGA-GOV-01-05), scored at 64.5% on an assumed 220M population (71% to 57% across 200M to 250M). TechCabal reports 117.5M issued NINs against 134.6M enrolments at 30 June, about 53% versus 61% on the same denominator (Red Team Finding 5).
- **Nigeria's internet figure** is NCC broadband penetration at 56.1% for May (NGA-INF-01-05); it counts subscriptions, not people, so it is not like-for-like with South Africa and Kenya.
- **Kenya's Maisha Namba coverage (GOV-01, 40.6%)** is a fifth week on a May-2024 base. Card issuance is active (1.7M+ cards printed) but no coverage statistic is published (KEN-GOV-01-06).
- **Nothing new** for UN e-government (the 2026 survey is scheduled but not found), AI talent (no 2026 PwC/Strategy& edition) or Kenya's FIN-02 (outside Chainalysis's published top 20).
