## What actually happened this week, even though no score moved

1. **Kenya's digital-ID figure (GOV-01, 40.6%) was traced to its root cause.** For two cycles,
   this figure rested on a May 2024 government statement that kept getting recycled by secondary
   press without an update. This week, per the prior cycle's recommendation, the primary source
   (immigration.go.ke) was checked directly — and it does not publish a consolidated coverage
   statistic at all. Three consecutive cycles have now failed to find a fresher number by any
   method. This reframes the problem: it's not that journalists are being lazy, it's that Kenya's
   government doesn't currently appear to publish this number anywhere findable.
2. **South Africa's and Kenya's missing crypto-adoption figure (FIN-02) is now understood
   structurally, not just as an unlucky search.** Chainalysis's own Sub-Saharan Africa regional
   report gives real, sourced context — South Africa is the region's #2 crypto market by dollar
   volume, Kenya is in the regional top five — but never states either country's position in the
   global 151-country ranked table, which is what this indicator's formula requires, and that
   full table is only published as an interactive map, not a fetchable document. Repeating this
   search next cycle is unlikely to produce a different result without a process change (see
   Red Team Finding 4 for options).
3. **Nigeria's corrected crypto-adoption rank (#6 of 151, from two cycles ago) held steady** —
   Chainalysis's current edition is unchanged, so no further correction was needed this week.

## What's working

- **Infrastructure, Government, and AI pillars are stable and well-corroborated** across all
  three countries — nothing in this week's re-checks contradicted the existing figures.
- **The FIN-01 cross-country construct problem** (Kenya: survey usage; Nigeria: registered
  accounts; South Africa: active accounts — three different things) remains accurately flagged
  rather than papered over. It's now been visible for three consecutive real-data weeks.

## What still needs fixing

- **FIN-01's construct inconsistency across the three countries** is, per the project's own
  priorities, the most significant open methodological issue, and this week's Red Team report
  recommends it move from a recurring note to an actual `METHODOLOGY.md` revision the next time
  there's room for non-weekly-run work.
- **FIN-02 for South Africa and Kenya** needs either better source access (Chainalysis's fuller
  "Geography of Crypto" report, beyond the free blog post) or a documented, lower-confidence
  fallback construct — see Red Team Finding 4 for the specific options on the table.
- **Kenya's GOV-01** will likely stay low-confidence until Kenya's government itself changes what
  it publishes; this is now flagged as a candidate for a future Society/digital-rights indicator
  rather than something a better search query can fix.
