# Integration Notes — Week of 2026-09-16

No link strengthened or weakened on new evidence this cycle; the notes below record where the existing links stand. Annotation only; no value changed.

## South Africa
The bank-as-identity-issuer link (GOV-01 <-> Finance) is unchanged in the evidence: about 700,000 Smart IDs issued via bank branches since March and a green-ID cut-off tied to financial-sector access (ZAF-GOV-01-06, carried forward from 2026-09-09, not re-checked). Chainalysis's 2026 index (ZAF-FIN-02-02: rank 9 of 117; 4th in domestic P2P, 3rd in cross-border) shows a large crypto market sitting beside a FIN-01 of 7.26 that cannot see bank/card/PayShap rails. The Finance pillar (45.4) now averages those two unlike constructs, so it describes neither well.

## Nigeria
Identity and connectivity continue to move together: NIMC enrolment above 142M (NGA-GOV-01-05) and NCC broadband penetration at 56.1% in May (NGA-INF-01-05). Crypto leadership is unchanged at rank 3 of 117, first in P2P and cross-border flows (NGA-FIN-02-05). The hypothesis from earlier cycles (easing naira moderates FIN-02 while ECO-02 recovers) is still untested: ECO-02 stays on FY2025 and the FIN-02 edition changed method, so neither side has a comparable new reading. Watch full-year 2026 funding data.

## Kenya
The GOV-01 -> FIN-01 chain still cannot be traced. M-Pesa usage (FIN-01 52.6, carried forward) stays independent of the state ID system. Digital-ID activity is visible (1.7M+ Maisha Cards printed, a 6M-ID budget request, free-ID policy) but none of it is a population coverage statistic (KEN-GOV-01-06); fifth week on the May-2024 base. The Kenya funding cooldown in H1 2026 (per 2026-09-09 evidence, not re-checked) points to a cooling, not strengthening, digital-economy link.
