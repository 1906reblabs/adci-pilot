# Integration Notes — Week of 2026-09-23

## South Africa
No new evidence this run. The 09-09 reading stands: banks issuing Smart IDs (about 700,000 since March, per DHA) and a green-ID cut-off tied to bank access mean government and finance share rails, while FIN-01's mobile-money lens (7.26) cannot see it. This note is carried forward, not re-tested.

## Nigeria
Connectivity and identity are rising together but the binding constraint is shifting. NCC broadband penetration reached 57.4% in July (NGA-INF-01-10) and NIMC enrolment holds at 142M (NGA-GOV-01-10). At NCC's Sept 29-30 forum, though, the regulator named usage rather than coverage as the larger gap: about 90% covered, about 27% with smartphones. If identity is meant to unlock services (NIN-linked finance, e-government), device ownership is the link to watch. GOV-02 (53.7, lowest of the three) is the same story from the government side. The naira/FIN-02/ECO-02 pairing remains untested: FIN-02 changed method and no full-year funding figure exists.

## Kenya
The GOV-01 -> FIN-01 chain still cannot be traced: a fifth week without a published Maisha Namba coverage figure (KEN-GOV-01-11, context only), though cards printed and backlog clearing continue per secondary reports. M-Pesa usage (FIN-01 52.6) stays independent of the state ID system. Kenya's FIN-02 is still missing (not in Chainalysis's published top 20). No new link appeared or weakened this week.
