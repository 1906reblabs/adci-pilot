# Integration Notes — Week of 2026-09-09

## South Africa
Two bank-linked threads strengthened this week. Home Affairs says bank branches have issued about
700,000 Smart IDs since March (ZAF-GOV-01-05, not scored), and it ties the coming green-ID cut-off
to financial-sector access: once the green book lapses, the Smart ID is the only document banks can
accept. At the same time Chainalysis's 2026 index puts South Africa 9th of 117 (4th in domestic
peer-to-peer, 3rd in cross-border; ZAF-FIN-02-01), which finally fills FIN-02. Government identity and
finance are sharing rails (banks as ID issuers) while the country's finance activity stays invisible
to FIN-01's mobile-money lens (7.26). The link is strengthening on the ground; the index still
cannot see it from the Finance side.

## Nigeria
Identity and connectivity are moving together: NIMC enrolment reached 142M (NGA-GOV-01-04) under the
NIMC Act 2026 and a presidential end-2026 target, while NCC broadband penetration rose to 56.1% in
May (NGA-INF-01-04). Stablecoin-driven crypto use keeps Nigeria at #3 globally, first in both P2P and
cross-border flows (NGA-FIN-02-04), and H1 2026 shows equity funding at $214M, the continental lead
(NGA-ECO-02-08, context only). Last cycle's hypothesis was that an easing naira would moderate FIN-02
while ECO-02 recovered. This cycle gives a partial test: ECO-02 context improved, but FIN-02 did
not moderate - rank improved to #3, although the index changed method and country set, so the
comparison is weak. A pairing is not confirmed or rejected. Watch the full-year 2026 funding numbers.

## Kenya
The GOV-01 -> FIN-01 chain still cannot be traced for Kenya. Digital-ID activity is visible
(1.5M first-time national IDs in five months, free-ID policy), but a June-2026 PS statement of
"34 million captured" describes the national ID register rather than Maisha Namba, and no coverage
statistic has been published for a fourth week (KEN-GOV-01-05 rejected). M-Pesa remains independent of
the state ID system. Kenya's FIN-02 is still missing: it is not in Chainalysis's published top 20.
With H1 2026 funding at its weakest since early 2021 (context only), the digital-economy link that
looked strongest in 2025 is cooling, not strengthening.
